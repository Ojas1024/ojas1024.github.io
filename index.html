01 · Research

Models that let go.

When a patient withdraws consent, the model has to forget them, and so does the preprocessing it learned from them.

PLU-GGN · machine unlearning

Tabular pipelines learn their imputation means, scales and encodings from data. I showed that a frozen pipeline keeps the deleted patients' statistics, and that standard influence updates can land further from retraining than doing nothing. PLU-GGN downdates the pipeline exactly and corrects the Newton step for the shifted coordinates.

  • 26×lower weight error than standard influence
  • 10−5distance to full retraining after two steps
  • ≈5×faster than retraining

TSAP · thermal vision-language

Plain captioning sees "a hot car". TSAP aims for "a running vehicle with combustion heat at the engine compartment". Regions come from heat-peak saliency and a zero-shot detector, SAM segments them, local physics is compressed into short tokens, and a single VLM call returns a structured diagnosis.

02 · Systems

Close to the metal.

Interpreters without heaps, databases without trade-offs, and a way back for files you thought were gone.

AetherOS Nano · edge OS for ESP32

An event-driven, dual-core operating system for microcontrollers with under 512 KB of SRAM. Its execution engine runs C, C++, Python and its own Aether language directly on the chip, with no host compiler, no flash writes and no heap allocation.

A database built from scratch in Python that keeps relational SQL, JSON documents, graphs and vector embeddings in one self-contained file, with multi-tenant isolation, plus a command-line client for scripting and exploration.

Lifeline · deleted-file recovery

A guided recovery app for Windows, macOS and Linux. It asks what happened, then reads back what NTFS, FAT and exFAT forgot, with original names and folders, and searches every block for 20 families of file formats when the records are gone. It also finds lost partitions and images failing drives, and it never writes to the drive it scans.

  • 20file format families recovered by content
  • ~1 GB/ssearched per worker process
  • 3operating systems, one codebase
